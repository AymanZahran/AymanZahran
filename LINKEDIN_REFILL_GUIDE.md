- **Profile completeness bar** (top of your profile) should reach **All-Star**. LinkedIn shows you what's missing.

- **Activity** → toggle on showcase posts and articles you've published; LinkedIn surfaces these in search.
- **Endorsements**: ask 5–10 close colleagues to endorse your top-3 skills (AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform).
- **Recommendations**: aim for 2–3 short written recommendations from past managers or peers.
